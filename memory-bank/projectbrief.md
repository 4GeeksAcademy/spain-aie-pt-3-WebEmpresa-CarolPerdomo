# Project Brief: Nexova Solutions

## Business

Nexova Solutions is a human resources consulting and talent acquisition firm founded in 2011. It is headquartered in Valencia, Spain, with an expansion office in Miami, Florida. The company has approximately 120 employees and annual revenue of about USD 8 million.

Its three business lines are:

- Executive and mid-management headhunting.
- Outsourced customer-support teams for technology, retail, and financial-services companies.
- Corporate training in soft skills and leadership.

Nexova serves primarily mid-sized companies. Its competitive strengths are its established candidate and client network, sector expertise, and experience operating in Spain and the United States.

## Project Goal

The current project milestone is Nexova's public website and talent-registration experience. The site should present the company and its services professionally, explain why clients choose Nexova, and give professionals a structured way to register their interest in current or future job opportunities.

The candidate experience must collect the fields and consent specified in `CONTEXT.md`, validate them accessibly, and provide a clear success state. Companies asking about Nexova's services need a separate, visible contact path; the candidate form must not be treated as a company-services lead form.

## Problem

Nexova's corporate website was built in 2019, is outdated and inaccessible, and does not reflect the company's current positioning. Candidate CVs arrive through unstructured email, making it difficult for the selection team to review profiles consistently. Across the wider business, recruitment, training, customer support, sales, HR, and executive reporting also rely on disconnected tools and manual work, limiting visibility and scale.

## Project Outcomes

- A responsive, accessible, search-optimized public website that reflects Nexova's B2B HR and talent services.
- A distinct candidate-registration flow with all required fields, domain-specific validation, useful errors, success feedback, and a working reset action.
- Accurate organization metadata and Schema.org markup.
- Clear separation between job-seeker registration and company inquiries.
- A maintainable foundation for later AI Engineering work such as CV ranking, candidate search, support automation, and operational dashboards.
